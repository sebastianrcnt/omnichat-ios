# OpenChat Support

## Getting help

Report a bug, ask a question or suggest a feature on
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sebastianrcnt/openchat-ios/issues). Please include your device, iOS or macOS version,
the app version and what kind of connection you use. Never post your API keys.

The app can also prepare a diagnostics report with keys and tokens removed: turn on Settings → Developer → Developer mode, then open Diagnostics.

## Common questions

**How do I start?** Open Settings → Connections → Add, then add an API key (OpenAI-compatible or Anthropic), sign in with
ChatGPT, or use Apple's on-device model where your device supports it.

**Does OpenChat cost anything?** The app is free. The model service you connect may charge you for usage under your own
account.

**Where are my chats stored?** On your device. Chats sync through your iCloud only if you turn it on in Settings → iCloud.

**Can I use a model on my own computer?** Yes. Add an OpenAI-compatible connection with its address (for example Ollama or
LM Studio on your Mac, or a server on your network).
