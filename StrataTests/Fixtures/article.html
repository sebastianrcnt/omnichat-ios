The Quiet Tide

By A. Writer

The tide rose slowly over the harbour, carrying with it the smell of salt and old rope. Fishermen gathered along the quay, mending nets and trading the small stories that sustain a working town through the long grey months of winter.

Nobody remembered exactly when the lighthouse had last been painted, but everyone agreed that the stripes were once red. The keeper, a patient man named Tobias, kept a ledger of every ship that passed, and every entry ended with the same two words: all well.

In spring the visitors came, and with them the questions. How old is the harbour wall? Who built the chapel? Tobias answered each one in the same unhurried voice, as if the answers were tides of their own, returning on schedule.
